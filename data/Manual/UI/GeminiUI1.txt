Here is a taxonomy of the logic games you provided, organized into seven distinct categories based on their primary solving mechanics.

## 1. Loop Construction
Games in this category focus on drawing a single, continuous, non-intersecting line or "loop" through the grid based on specific constraints.

* **Dotchi-Loop**
* **Hebi**
* **Masyu**
* **Mid-loop**
* **Slitherlink**
* **Suraromu**

## 2. Cell Shading & Deduction
These puzzles require you to determine which cells should be shaded (blackened) and which should remain white, often ensuring shaded cells don't touch or that white cells remain connected.

* **Heyawake**
* **Hitori**
* **Kurodoko**
* **Norinori**
* **Nurikabe**
* **Nuritwin**
* **Sto-stone**
* **Usowan**

## 3. Region Partitioning & Polyominoes
The core mechanic here involves dividing the grid into smaller shapes or placing specific geometric figures (like polyominoes) into pre-defined regions.

* **Chained Block**
* **Choco Banana**
* **Double Choco**
* **Evolomino**
* **Fillomino**
* **Five Cells**
* **LITS**
* **Shikaku**

## 4. Numerical Placement
Solve these by filling the grid cells with digits. These games rely on arithmetic constraints, sequences, or the "Latin Square" principle where numbers cannot repeat in a given set.

* **Juosan**
* **Makaro**
* **Nansuke**
* **Ripple Effect**
* **Sudoku**

## 5. Pathfinding & Connectivity
Rather than a closed loop, these games require connecting nodes or areas using paths, bridges, or lines that do not necessarily return to their starting point.

* **Hashiwokakero**
* **Nagareru**
* **Numberlink**
* **Pencils**
* **Sashigane**

## 6. Direction & Visibility Logic
These puzzles use arrows, "light," or line-of-sight mechanics to dictate how far an influence travels across the grid or where specific markers must be placed.

* **Akari**
* **Nurimeizu**
* **Nurimisaki**
* **Shakashaka**
* **Yajilin**

## 7. Object Movement & Area Fulfillment
These games involve moving "pieces" into specific spots or filling certain areas of the grid until specific count-based or regional criteria are met.

* **Kurotto**
* **Moon-or-Sun**
* **Satogaeri**
* **Tilepaint**
* **Yosenabe**
